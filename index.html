SOURCE & SCOPE

About these samples

The source conversations come from the MSA benchmark, constructed from LibriSpeech recordings. The Qwen3-TTS and X-VC versions were recovered from the same fixed 64-session development experiment. Three paired sessions were selected per speaker count; all recovered files were checked for presence and valid WAV format.

The original source WAVs were converted from 16-kHz float to 16-kHz PCM for browser playback; no new model inference or rescoring was performed. The separate official MSA section links to the authors’ own audio, using different conversation IDs. This page is a listening supplement, not a substitute for the paper’s full-set VPC2026 privacy/utility results or official MSA diarization protocol.

Audio attribution: MSA benchmark by Xiaoxiao Miao et al.; LibriSpeech by Vassil Panayotov et al. Please consult the upstream terms before reusing the audio. Website code and future model-release materials are tracked separately from the audio assets.
